4. Do not copy, rewrite, or combine ranking-work titles, character relationships, concrete plots, distinctive dialogue, or story structures. Do not promise revenue, media-buying results, or success probability.
5. decision.selected_hypothesis_ids must exactly match report_ref. Select only hypotheses explicitly chosen by the user. All four guardrails booleans must be true.
